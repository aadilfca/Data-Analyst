Cash and Bank voucher selection

Account Control Centre is the central source. Assign each Cash or Bank Trial Balance account here; the selected account code identifies the Treasury leg, including a Journal Voucher that contains that account.

Payment
The selected Cash/Bank account is the credit leg. Its debit counterpart line(s) are the payment side.
Receipt
The selected Cash/Bank account is the debit leg. Its credit counterpart line(s) are the receipt side.

Treasury remains first: a voucher containing a configured Cash or Bank leg stays in Cash/Bank Verification. A Cash-to-Bank transfer can appear in the relevant Treasury workstreams.
