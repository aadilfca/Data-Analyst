Terms of use

Last updated: 20 October 2026

These terms describe the responsibilities of authorised users of Internal Audit App.

Authorised access

Use the app only with an account and permissions provided by the administrator, for legitimate audit and related business work. Keep credentials confidential and report suspected unauthorised access to the administrator.

Records and confidentiality

Upload, view and share only information you are authorised to handle. Respect client confidentiality, applicable requirements and your organisation's procedures. Review the declared period and validation results before importing a replacement report.

Review of outputs

Reports and checks support professional review. The responsible team must assess source completeness, configuration, reconciliations and evidence before relying on an output or issuing an audit conclusion.

Connected services and archives

An authorised administrator manages Google Drive consent, storage access and archive retention. Google Drive, hosting and database services operate under their own terms. Service interruptions or revoked permissions can prevent operations that require a verified archive.

Privacy, support and updates

The privacy policy explains information handling. Contact aadil.aca@gmail.com for support, access or data requests. Updates to these terms are published here with a revised date.
