Account-driven Verification configuration

Voucher-prefix configuration is retired. Verification workstreams are assigned centrally in Account Control Centre.

  • Assign Cash vouchers, Bank vouchers, Local vendor, Import vendor, Export customer, Payroll, and Contractor accounts in the Verification accounts tab.
  • Cash and Bank voucher selection uses the account codes assigned there; no CPV, BPV, CRV, BRV, or JV prefix setting is required.
  • Existing voucher-prefix rows are retained only for historical compatibility.
