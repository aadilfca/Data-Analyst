📖 How the app identifies voucher types

  • Category — including whether it's a Payment or a Receipt — is decided directly by which prefix list the voucher number matches. For example, if "CPV" is listed under Cash Payment, any voucher number starting with CPV is treated as a Cash Payment voucher. There is no separate debit/credit lookup for this.
  • Journal (JV) has no Payment/Receipt split. Every journal entry is simply tagged as a Journal Voucher, regardless of debit or credit.
  • A voucher number that doesn't match any prefix in any list below is not treated as a Cash, Bank, or Journal voucher.
