This policy explains how Internal Audit App processes information for authorised audit work.

Records we process and why

The app processes the records users upload or enter, including account details, vouchers, dates, amounts, descriptions, supporting evidence and audit observations. It also processes user account details, access roles and session information.

This information is used to validate and maintain source records, support audit reviews, prepare reports and control access. A necessary session cookie keeps signed-in users authenticated.

Storage and access

Information is held in the database and file storage configured for this deployment. The administrator can provide details about the hosting and storage providers used here. Those providers may process technical service logs to operate their services.

Client records are intended for authorised users with access assigned by the administrator. Administrators responsible for hosting, storage and connected services may also have access needed to operate and support the app. Public information pages do not display client records.

Retention and removal

The administrator manages retention according to the organisation's audit, business and applicable legal requirements. This policy does not set a fixed retention period.

Information may remain in backups or audit history according to the deployment's configured retention process. Ask the administrator how retention and removal requests are handled.

Your choices and requests

Contact the administrator to request access, correction or deletion of information. Requests are reviewed against access permissions, audit obligations and retention requirements. Only upload information you are authorised to provide.

Contact and policy updates

The application administrator can be contacted at aadil.aca@gmail.com. Updates are published on this page with a revised date.
