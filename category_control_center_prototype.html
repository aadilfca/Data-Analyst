Knowledge base overview

This category covers purchases that require an authorised demand, approved order, receiving evidence, and a vendor bill before the voucher line can be cleared.

!
Configuration principleEvidence defines what must be present; procedures and cross-checks explain how it is reviewed.

Audit focus

  • Prevent unauthorised or unsupported purchases.
  • Compare quantities and rates across the document chain.
  • Keep document selection explicit and visible to the reviewer.
  • Keep informational authority metadata separate from status gates.
